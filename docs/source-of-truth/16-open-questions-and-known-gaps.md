# Open Questions and Known Gaps

## Purpose

This document records unresolved architecture evidence, business-rule
questions, implementation/business mismatches, incomplete evidence, and
technical debt that still requires confirmation. Do not add speculative issues
merely to populate the register.

Use the evidence, status, and fact-type rules in
[`00-README.md`](./00-README.md).

## Recording Rules

- State exactly what is unknown or conflicting.
- Distinguish an OPEN QUESTION from a verified KNOWN LIMITATION.
- Cite the evidence reviewed and what evidence is still missing.
- Identify affected modules without assigning an owner or deadline unless
  confirmed.
- Close an entry only with evidence; preserve a concise resolution note when
  historical context matters.
- Do not use this register to propose unapproved product features.
